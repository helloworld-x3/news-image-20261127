## 1. Thanksgiving storm batters US East Coast (lead)
- Storm hit the US East Coast on Thanksgiving Day, 26 Nov; power out to
  836,000+ customers by Thursday afternoon, ~900,000 by the evening (AP);
  417 US flights cancelled, ~1,800 delayed (FlightAware); gusts to 70 mph
  (112 km/h); the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade postponed — the
  first weather postponement in its history; New Jersey statewide emergency.
- Source: Reuters, "Thanksgiving storm knocks out power, disrupts flights
  on US East Coast",

## 2. Tokyo inflation stays hot at 2.9%
- Tokyo core CPI +2.9% y/y in November, data released Friday 27 Nov (JST);
  vs 3.0% forecast; above the BOJ's 2% target for a third straight month;
  core-core +2.5% (was 2.6%); strengthens the case for a hike at the BOJ
  meeting ending 18 December.
- Source: Reuters, "Core inflation in Japan's capital accelerates in
  November",
  https://www.reuters.com/markets/asia/core-inflation-japans-capital-accelerates-november-2026-11-26/
- Photo: Bank of Japan headquarters, Tokyo. Public domain.

## 3. Diesel tanker capsizes off Ibiza; one dead
- Malta-flagged chemical tanker Med Emre capsized ~30 nm (56 km) off Ibiza
  early Thursday 26 Nov, Algeciras to Barcelona, up to 7,000 tonnes of
  diesel. All 17 crew rescued; one later died in hospital, six minor
  injuries (mostly hypothermia). Spain activated its maritime pollution
  plan at level 2; no major spill found, small sheens observed.
- Source: Reuters, "Crew member dies after diesel tanker sinks off Ibiza;
  Spain launches pollution plan",
  https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/crew-member-dies-after-diesel-tanker-sinks-off-ibiza-spain-launches-pollution-2026-11-26/

## 4. Delhi police free 164 in 'cyber slavery' raid
- Delhi Police Crime Branch rescued 164 foreign nationals (92 Indonesian,
  72 Malaysian) from a call centre in Noida; fake job offers, passports
  seized, forced to run investment scams. One arrest (Tan Guan Wei, 25,
  Malaysian). Seized: 355 phones, 168 desktops, 26 laptops, 53 SIM cards,
  174 passports. Police announced it Thursday 26 Nov; widely reported
  Friday 27 Nov.
- Source: NDTV, "Delhi Police Bust International Cyber Slavery Racket,
  Rescue 164 Foreigners" (27 Nov 2026),
  https://www.ndtv.com/delhi-news/delhi-police-bust-international-cyber-slavery-racket-rescue-164-foreigners-12382653
  (corroborated by Deccan Herald, 27 Nov 05:46 IST).
